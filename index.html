Tema 1 • Especialización Académica

Technical English 🛠️

El lenguaje de precisión utilizado en la ciencia, la investigación tecnológica y los entornos profesionales avanzados.

Concept & Definition

"Technical English is a variety of English used in specific professional, academic, scientific, and technological fields, such as engineering, medicine, computer science, technology, economics, and finance."

It is not limited to learning general grammar. It also includes vocabulary, expressions, and structures related to a particular profession or area of study.

En español: El inglés técnico es una variedad utilizada específicamente por profesionales académicos, científicos y tecnológicos. Va más allá de las reglas generales de gramática, enfocándose en el léxico e instrucciones específicas de cada campo.

Importance 💡

Why Technical English is essential for postgraduate students:

  • ✅
    Understand specialized documents:

    Helps understand manuals, technical sheets, and operational protocols.

  • ✅
    Read academic & scientific papers:

    Facilitates literature reviews, peer-reviewed journals, and thesis references.

  • ✅
    Use technological tools & equipment:

    Assists in configuring software, hardware systems, and laboratory instruments.

  • ✅
    International collaboration:

    Promotes clear communication among researchers and teams worldwide.

  • ✅
    Career & academic growth:

    Significantly increases professional opportunities and scholarships.

  • ✅
    Updated scientific updates:

    Provides direct access to the latest breakthroughs in science and technology.

Characteristics 🎯

Core linguistic pillars of Technical English:

Precision

Uses words with specific, unambiguous meanings.

Clarity

Presents information in an easy-to-understand and direct way.

Conciseness

Expresses ideas with few words, without unnecessary filler.

Objectivity

Focuses strictly on facts, processes, and measurable results.

Specialized Vocab

Employs terms and nomenclature tied to particular disciplines.

Instructions

Step-by-step guidance on how to perform activities or use gear.

Acronyms

Integrates standardized abbreviations commonly used in professions.

Formal Tone

Mainly applied in structured academic and technical publications.

Tema 2 • Comunicación Dinámica

Interactive English 🗣️

El uso activo del inglés para dialogar, intercambiar perspectivas y participar fluidamente en el mundo real.

Concept & Definition

"Interactive English is the use of English to communicate actively with other people. It includes speaking, listening, asking questions, answering, expressing opinions, sharing ideas, and participating in conversations."

It can be used in face-to-face conversations, chats, video calls, debates, interviews, role-plays, and group activities.

👥 Face-to-Face 💬 Online Chats 📹 Video Calls 🎙️ Debates & Symposia 🎭 Role-Plays
En español: El inglés interactivo es el inglés aplicado para comunicarse activamente en situaciones reales. Abarca no solo la teoría, sino la interacción bidireccional, negociación de significados y trabajo en equipo.

Importance & Real-Life Benefits ✨

Real-Life Situations

Helps people communicate smoothly in authentic daily scenarios.

Speaking & Listening

Develops sharp comprehension and clear verbal articulation.

Boosts Confidence

Empowers students to overcome the psychological barrier and fear of speaking.

Teamwork & Fluency

Encourages cooperation, active participation, and natural rhythm.

Tema 3 • Metodología Pedagógica

Importance of Interactive English Classes 🎓

"English classes should be interactive at every educational level because students need to practice the language, not only memorize grammar rules."

🌱
Foundational Stage

At the Basic Level

A1 - A2

Interactive activities help students build core linguistic confidence and overcome the fear of speaking:

  • Greetings and farewells: Good morning, See you soon, Take care.
  • Personal introductions: Name, profession, background, academic interest.
  • Numbers and colors: Counts, percentages, technical specs, visual identifiers.
  • Simple questions: What is this? Where is the file? How does it work?
  • Everyday vocabulary: Essential terms for daily social and academic interaction.
  • Basic sentences: Subject + Verb + Complement structures.
Main Goal: Help students overcome their fear of speaking English and establish initial conversational instincts.
🚀
Advanced Academic Stage

At the Intermediate Level

B1 - B2+

Students move beyond basic survival speech and transition into critical academic analysis:

  • Describe people, places & experiences: Articulating detailed contexts, fieldwork, and team dynamics.
  • Express opinions & critical arguments: Justifying methodological choices, hypotheses, and solutions.
  • Participate actively in activities: Leading debates, academic seminars, and symposium discussions.
  • Negotiation of meaning: Clarifying ambiguous points and resolving complex inquiries.
  • Synthesize technical texts: Summarizing research findings and articulating conclusions verbally.
  • Fluency & Spontaneity: Conversing without prolonged pauses or excessive mental translation.
Main Goal: Achieve professional autonomy and academic defense capabilities in bilingual environments.
